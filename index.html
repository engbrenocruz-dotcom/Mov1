How does an open belt transmit power?

Model assumptions

Two equal 210 mm pulleys are 360 mm apart. The driver runs at 600 rpm; an open belt turns the driven pulley in the same direction. Initial tension is 400 N per strand, contact angle π radians and axial stiffness EA = 60 kN. A load ramp increases torque from 30% to the selected value. T₁ − T₂ = torque/radius, while T₁ + T₂ ≈ 800 N. The capstan inequality T₁/T₂ ≤ exp(µθ) limits the available torque. Elastic creep is approximated by (T₁ − T₂)/EA. If demand exceeds the friction limit, a simple 2 kg·m² driven-inertia model slows the output and can stall it; gross slip is not hidden. Belt mass, centrifugal tension, temperature, bending losses and detailed creep mechanics are omitted. Rotation is displayed 20× slower; the physics clock remains real time. The input/output power difference is reported as an illustrative loss estimate.
